## Locked title

The two-point strain–direction pairing on unmodified Navier–Stokes ends at \( C_{\mathrm{CZ}} M(t)\,|\delta\xi|^2 + \text{remainders} \). Replacing the coefficient by an \( M \)-independent \( -\gamma \) is equivalent to the regularity problem.

## Explicit non-claim

This note does not prove global regularity of the 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations. It does not discharge the Constantin–Fefferman hypothesis. It records the precise stopping point of the standard geometric kernel expansion on the unmodified equations.
